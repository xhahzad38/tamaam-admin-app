How receiving works

  1. 1
    Check in at the dock

    When the truck or courier arrives, open the shipment (scan the ASN or a carton label in the search box) and scan each carton label. Cartons not scanned are recorded as missing.

  2. 2
    Count the units

    Open each carton and scan every unit's FNSKU label. Switch to damaged mode for units that cannot be sold. Save progress at any time.

  3. 3
    Close the shipment

    Sellable units go live for sale straight away. Anything not counted is closed as a discrepancy and the seller is notified.
